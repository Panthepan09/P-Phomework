How Team Cherry make Hollow Knight(and then Silk Song)?

Way before the concept of Hollow Knight exist, TC attended a indie game competition called Ludum Dare and created Hungry Knight. And that's the prototype of Hollow Knight.

Later, TC wants to make a new game, and they choose the little knight as lead role, again.


The two developers all like to play Rockman, a game created and pubulished by CAPCOM. This game deeply influenced Hollow Knight, like Movement Physics and hardcore punishments.

Origin of Silk Song

Before developing Hollow Knight, Team Cherry needed funding, so they launched an online funding. One of the goals was to unlock a second playable character, which was Hornet. However, as more and more content was added during development, TC decided to expand it from a DLC (Downloadable Content, an additional software extension released after a game's launch) into a full-fledged sequel.
